# 🥷 Criminal Simulator Hub (by sega1games12-alt)

A clean, optimized, and safe hub for **Criminal Simulator [ALPHA]**.

## ✨ Features:
- **👁️ Loot ESP** — Highlights Gold, Bags, Rings, and Safes (with distance-based streaming to prevent lag).
- **👮 NPC ESP** — See Police (Blue), Guards (Red), and Civilians (Green) through walls.
- **✈️ Fly** — Smooth flight (WASD to move, Space to go up, Shift to go down).
- **🚶 NoClip** — Walk through walls to get loot faster.
- **🎨 Premium UI** — Draggable, auto-sizing window with 5 themes (Gold, Red, Blue, Green, Rainbow).
- **🌐 Multi-language** — Fully translated into English, Russian, and Ukrainian.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer
The game has a **very strict server-side anti-cheat** against teleporting and money injection. 
- Do NOT spam Fly/NoClip for long distances, or you might get teleported back.
- This script is for **educational purposes only**. Use at your own risk. I am not responsible for any bans.
